The Comic Project • Viral Ambassador Surge

We Are Onboarding 5,000 Volunteers.

The volunteers are social media ambassadors. We will teach them how to edit and post videos. We give them pre-edited CapCut video template files, they record a short clip of themselves, insert it into the file, and post at a specific synchronized time tagging our social media handles.

CapCut Video Templates

We supply pre-edited video files and teach you video editing. Just drop in your 5-second selfie and publish!

3×3 Viral Multiplication

Each volunteer is to influence and onboard 3 people. All the onboarded will onboard another 3 people each!

$1/Mo & Daily Web Comic

Each agrees to make a $1 donation monthly (≈₦1,250) to fund slum schooling while enjoying the daily web comic.
