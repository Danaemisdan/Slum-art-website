Transforming Underserved Communities Through Creative Art & Education

Slum Art Foundation is a registered non-profit organization dedicated to transforming underserved communities through art, education, technology, creativity, and grassroots action. We believe talent exists in every community, regardless of economic circumstances.

Our Vision

To build a world where every child and young person, regardless of where they come from or their socioeconomic circumstances, has the opportunity to discover their talent, develop their creative potential, and build a meaningful, self-reliant future.

Our Mission

To use art, creativity, education, technology, and community action to empower underserved communities, unlock human potential, create economic opportunities, and develop sustainable solutions to social challenges—starting in Africa and reaching communities around the world.

Founder & Lead Visionary

Adetunwase Adenle

Social Entrepreneur • Master Artist • Innovator • 4× Guinness World Records™ Holder

Adetunwase Adenle is a social entrepreneur, artist, innovator, and community development advocate passionate about using art, education, technology, and creativity to transform underserved communities across Nigeria.

He is the Founder of Slum Art Foundation and the visionary behind initiatives such as the Slum Art PET Bottle School. As a four-time Guinness World Records holder—including orchestrating the largest painting in the world created by the greatest number of children—Adetunwase has dedicated his career to taking street youth off hazardous shoreline trash labor and placing them into accredited classrooms and fine-art studios.

His vision is to use creativity and sustainable innovation to create lasting social impact across Africa and underserved communities around the world.

Eco-Architectural Innovation

The Slum Art PET Bottle School

Located in Ijora Badia, Lagos, Nigeria, the Slum Art PET Bottle School is an internationally acclaimed learning center constructed entirely from thousands of recycled plastic bottles, discarded bottle caps, compacted earth, and sand. Instead of allowing plastic waste to choke local lagoons and drainage canals, Adetunwase and community children converted shoreline trash into insulated, flood-resilient classrooms with solar power and natural airflow.

Empowerment Ecosystem

13 Creative & Skills Programs

We use creativity as a multifaceted catalyst for personal discovery, technical skill acquisition, and community self-reliance.

Visual Arts

Slum Art

Painting, drawing, sculpture, and recycling art. Teaches children to transform discarded plastics, glass, and papers into museum-grade art while fostering environmental conservation.

Music & Audio

Slum Music

Discovering and nurturing musical talent in underserved communities. Participants explore singing, songwriting, instrument performance, audio production, and storytelling.

Cinematography

Slum Film

Teaching young people visual storytelling through film. Participants learn directing, scriptwriting, cinematography, acting, and editing while documenting authentic community stories.

Performance

Slum Dance

Developing creativity, discipline, self-confidence, teamwork, and performance craft through contemporary and traditional African dance choreographies.

Visual Media

Slum Photography

Equipping youth with cameras and phones to communicate stories through images. Teaches visual documentation, lighting, and composition from their own lived perspective.

Urban Sports

Slum Skate

Engaging young people in positive urban recreation, balance, resilience, and discipline through skateboarding in a dedicated, safe community park.

Spoken Word

Slum Poetry

Giving young people a powerful platform to express their dreams, struggles, and hopes. Enhances writing, public speaking, and literacy skills to advocate for social change.

Sustainable Fashion

Slum Fashion

Introducing young people to fashion design, pattern cutting, tailoring, textiles, and waste upcycling, converting discarded fabrics into wearable art and viable livelihood.

Digital Storytelling

Slum Animation

Teaching 2D/3D animation, character design, visual effects, and AI-assisted creative tools, connecting traditional African folklore with modern digital media production.

Dramatic Arts

Slum Theatre

Acting, scriptwriting, stage production, and live theatre. Cultivates leadership, public presence, and empathy while staging original local plays.

Design & Branding

Slum Designs

Graphic design, product branding, illustration, and creative problem-solving, turning raw artistic concepts into commercial digital and physical products.

Technology & STEM

Slum Tech

Introducing young minds to coding, robotics, artificial intelligence, software design, and digital literacy to bridge the digital divide and prepare for high-tech careers.

Community Outreach

Slum Evangelism

Spreading compassion, moral character, hope, and community service through uplifting gatherings, creative music, and neighborhood outreach programs.

Grassroots Action

Community & Welfare Initiatives

Beyond classroom arts, our community initiatives deliver daily nourishment, health dignity, and joy directly into families' lives.

🍲

Food Clinic

Providing hot nutritious meals, clean water, and nutritional support to children and families living on the waterfront who struggle with daily food security.

✂️

Hair-Port

Community grooming initiative providing free haircuts, hairstyling, and hygiene care to street children, restoring dignity, self-worth, and self-confidence.

🎉

Jollof Rice Party

A vibrant community celebration of food, games, live performances, and laughter, bringing local families, donors, and scholars together in pure joy.

🛡️

Art-surance

A sustainable giving model connecting artwork acquisitions directly to medical checkups, health insurance, and emergency educational escrow for youth artists.

🤝

Mentorship

One-on-one personal and professional guidance pairing youth scholars with experienced artists, tech mentors, educators, and leaders to shape their future.

🌾

Garri Fiesta

A joyful cultural culinary festival celebrating Nigerian heritage through local foods, storytelling, traditional games, and intergenerational bonding.
