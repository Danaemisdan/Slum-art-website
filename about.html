Who We Are

Our Story, Vision & Leadership

Transforming underserved communities across Africa through art, education, technology, and community development.

Our Vision

To build a world where every child and young person, regardless of where they come from or their socioeconomic circumstances, has the opportunity to discover their talent, develop their creative potential, and build a meaningful, self-reliant future.

Our Mission

To use art, creativity, education, technology, and community action to empower underserved communities, unlock human potential, create economic opportunities, and develop sustainable solutions to social challenges—starting in Africa and reaching communities around the world.

Founder & Lead Visionary

Adetunwase Adenle

Social Entrepreneur • Artist • Innovator • 4-Time Guinness World Records™ Holder

Adetunwase Adenle is a passionate teacher and community advocate dedicated to bridging educational inequality in Nigeria. Born with an intense creative calling, he realized early that conventional schooling often leaves slum children behind due to poverty and lack of infrastructure.

He holds four official Guinness World Records—including orchestrating the largest painting in the world created by the greatest number of children. Through his visionary leadership, thousands of children have discovered their creative gifts and accessed education.

Eco-Architectural Innovation

The Slum Art Pet Bottle School

Located in the heart of Lagos, Nigeria, the Pet Bottle School is an internationally acclaimed structure built using thousands of recycled plastic bottles, discarded bottle caps, and sand. Instead of letting plastic trash pollute local lagoons and canals, Adenle and community youth upcycled the waste into durable, insulated walls for a bright learning hub.
